What We Offer

Our Services

Everything you need to find, book, and manage parking — for drivers and mall operators alike.

For Drivers

How To Find a Parking Spot

Use our real-time map to locate available parking slots across Kerala's top malls. Filter by vehicle type, time, and location.

1
Open the SMARTPARK app and select your preferred district or mall.
2
Browse available slots filtered by vehicle type and time window.
3
Reserve your slot, pay via wallet, and receive a QR code for entry.
For Malls

How To Rent Your Parking Spot

Register your mall and list your parking capacity. Set rates, manage slot availability, and earn from every booking made through SMARTPARK.

1
Register your mall and get approved by the SMARTPARK admin team.
2
Configure your parking slots, vehicle types, and pricing rates.
3
Assign supervisors, go live, and start accepting bookings immediately.
